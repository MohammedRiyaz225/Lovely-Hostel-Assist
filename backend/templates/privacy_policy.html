Privacy Policy for HostelFix

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HostelFix ("HostelSense", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and related services collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our hostel complaint management system at Lovely Professional University (LPU), Punjab.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, university student email address, phone number, designated hostel block (e.g., BH-1, GH-2), and room number.
  • Precise Location Data (GPS): With your explicit permission, we collect your approximate and precise location coordinates while using the application to verify that you are physically present within your designated hostel block perimeters. Location data is collected only while the app is actively in use (foreground) and is never tracked in the background.
  • Camera & Photo Evidence: If you choose to attach an image to your complaint, the app requests access to your camera or gallery to upload photo evidence of the defect or maintenance issue.
  • Device & Notification Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver push notifications regarding updates, assignments, and resolution of your complaints.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify residence within designated hostel blocks and prevent fraudulent or duplicate complaint submissions.
  • To categorize, prioritize, and route complaints to the appropriate university maintenance departments (Electrical, Civil, Plumbing, Carpentry, IT Infrastructure).
  • To notify you about changes in the status of your reported issues.
  • To collect student feedback ratings to improve hostel facilities and maintenance turnaround time.

4. Data Sharing & Security

Your data is strictly used for internal university hostel management purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers. All passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing, and API communication occurs over secure encrypted protocols (HTTPS/JWT).

5. Your Choices & Account Deletion

You can revoke location and camera permissions at any time through your Android device settings. You may request account deletion or data correction by contacting the hostel warden administration or via email at support@hostelfix.lpu.in.
